SparklingTones news 🎸

🎛️ Live mode on the computer (beta)
Your presets as big tiles, like a pedalboard. Click one and it plays.

🦶 Use your MIDI footswitch (beta)
Works with standard MIDI pedals like the M-Wave Chocolate, over USB or Bluetooth: press "Learn a switch", step on it, done. Not for dedicated Spark controllers like Control X or AirStep, which connect straight to the amp.
It's beta because I can't test every pedal out there. If you try it, tell me which one you used and how it went 🙏

💻 Windows app coming soon
In review on the Microsoft Store right now. Until then it runs in Chrome or Edge.
