## English

**Short description**
Organise the presets of your Positive Grid Spark 2 amplifier and play them live, over Bluetooth. Free, no account.

**Description**
Requires a Positive Grid Spark 2 or Spark NEO amplifier, connected over Bluetooth.

SparklingTones is a free, unofficial app for Positive Grid's Spark 2 and Spark NEO amplifiers.

• Preset library on your computer: categories, tags, notes, search.
• Effects chain editor: amps, pedals and knobs, with the sound changing as you turn them.
• Banks of eight sounds and a Live view with big buttons, also from a MIDI footswitch.
• Scenes (up to three per preset) and setlists for the gig.
• The amp's eight slots always in view, with nothing lost when you overwrite them.
• Tuner, tap tempo, preset import and export, backup.
• "Generate with AI": prepares a text to paste into your favourite assistant; the app does not connect to any AI service.
• Optional sync with your own Dropbox.

No account, no server: your presets stay on your computer.

SparklingTones is not affiliated with Positive Grid. Spark is a trademark of its respective owners.

**What's new in this version** (1.7.0)
• Scenes: up to three per preset, plus the Base; they change knobs and active effects without changing preset.
• Setlists: the songs of the night, each with its preset and scene.
• Recommended pickups for your guitar.
• Long press on MIDI switches can also be set to 0.7 s.
• A preset with an effect the amp doesn't know no longer plays.
